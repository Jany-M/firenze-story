| 2026-11-27 | Lungarni ([LUNGARNI.md](docs/landmarks/LUNGARNI.md)): the street is now a street – a narrow brick parapet, the road (stone paving, asphalt from 1950, the Acciaiuoli in stone again from 2020, the Archibusieri always stone), a 2 m pavement with a kerb along the houses (from 1870); the ground under it is held below the road.
