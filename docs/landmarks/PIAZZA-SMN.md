## What the model shows
- **The obelisks of the Corsa dei Cocchi**: two steps, a pietra forte pedestal with red Levanto panels, four bronze
  tortoises at the corners carrying the veined marble shaft, the bronze lily on top. Designed by Ammannati in 1570,
  brought from the quarry in 1572, set up in 1608 for the wedding of Cosimo II; the lilies and the panels renewed in the
  1790s. From 1563 to 1608 two wooden pyramids marked the turning posts of the chariot race (Wikipedia, "Obelisks of the
  Corsa dei Cocchi"; visitflorence; photographs Francesco Bini 2021, Zairon 2023).
- **The lawns**: flat beds flush with the paving behind a thin stone edge, no hedges; **six long low benches** across the axis
  in the middle of the square (two with wooden seats).
  The square is bare in the 19th-century photographs (Sommer, Brogi); the lawns are 20th-century – the year (1930) is
  *inferred*, as are the benches' places.
