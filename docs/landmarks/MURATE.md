# Le Murate

Model: `blender/scripts/build_murate.py` → `web/public/models/landmarks/murate.glb`, in absolute scene coordinates, geometry from
`pipeline/47_moderni.py` → `blender/data/moderni.json`. Register row [M36](../DATA-SOURCES.md#M36). Library page: [/library/murate/](/library/murate/).

Convent, prison and today a quarter of houses, cafés and squares between Via Ghibellina and Via dell'Agnolo.

## Measured
| Item | Value | Source |

## What the model shows
The wings massed from the DSM as they stand today: plaster walls, windows on each floor, hipped roofs round the courtyards.

## Phases
| Years | Part | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1370s | recluse women live by a pier of the Rubaconte bridge | [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monastero_delle_Murate) |
| 1424 | the monastery on Via Ghibellina, funded by Giovanni de' Benci (shown from 1424, *inferred* for the whole outline) | en.wikipedia; [FAI](https://fondoambiente.it/luoghi/carcere-le-murate) |
| 1845 / 1883 | turned into a prison (works from 1832 or 1845); the city's prison 1883–1985 | FAI; [visittuscany.com](https://www.visittuscany.com/it/attrazioni/le-murate-di-firenze/); [immobiliare.it](https://www.immobiliare.it/news/vivere-a/firenze/dove-si-trovano-le-murate-di-firenze-e-qual-e-la-loro-storia-398033/) |
| 2001– | recovered on a plan by Renzo Piano | [italia.it](https://www.italia.it/it/toscana/firenze/complesso-delle-murate-di-firenze) |

## Where the sources disagree
- **When the prison began:** works from 1832 (immobiliare.it), 1845 (FAI); prison from 1883 (visittuscany.com, en.wikipedia).
- **When it closed:** 1984 (immobiliare.it) or 1985 (en.wikipedia, visittuscany.com).
