# Palazzo della Borsa

Model: `blender/scripts/build_borsa.py` → `web/public/models/landmarks/borsa.glb`, frame from `pipeline/47_moderni.py borsa`
→ `blender/data/moderni.json`. Register row [M42](../DATA-SOURCES.md#M42). Library page: [/library/borsa/](/library/borsa/).

The Chamber of Commerce's palace on the Lungarno Diaz, between the Uffizi and the Ponte alle Grazie.

## What the model shows
- The long front of three storeys in pale yellow plaster with stone window frames and cornices over the main floor's windows,
  a rusticated ground floor in travertine, string courses, the main cornice and a low tiled roof.
- The pronaos in the middle: four Doric columns between two antae, entablature with triglyphs and a pediment over the
  cornice, steps up to it.
- The glass roofs over the light well and the hall.
