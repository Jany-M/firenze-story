# Stazione Leopolda

Model: `blender/scripts/build_leopolda.py` → `web/public/models/landmarks/leopolda.glb`, in absolute scene coordinates, geometry from
`pipeline/47_moderni.py` → `blender/data/moderni.json`. Register row [M34](../DATA-SOURCES.md#M34). Library page: [/library/leopolda/](/library/leopolda/).

Florence's first railway terminus, just outside the walls at Porta al Prato.

## Measured
| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Outline | ≈155 × 43 m | [OSM way 269666091](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/269666091) |
| Heights | the hall's roof ≈10 m, the head ≈8 m; ground 4.2 m (scene) | LiDAR 2023 DSM |

## What the model shows
- The long hall massed from the DSM with hipped roofs, plaster walls and windows.
- On the head towards Porta al Prato, a row of seven arched openings and a string course (*inferred* from the photographs of
  1850 and 1861).

## Phases
| Years | Part | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1848 | opened 2 June 1848, by Enrico Presenti for Grand Duke Leopold II | [Museo Galileo](https://brunelleschi.imss.fi.it/itineraries/place/FormerLeopoldaStationOfFlorenceMuseumOfRailwayObjects.html), [getbacklauretta.com](https://getbacklauretta.com/2018/06/12/florence-in-1861) |
| 1860 | passenger trains move to the central station after the link of 24 April 1860 | [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa%E2%80%93Florence_railway) |
| 1861 | the National Exhibition; later workshops; today an events hall | [facarospauls.com](https://facarospauls.com/apps/florence-art-and-culture/4383/stazione-leopolda), [feelflorence.it](https://feelflorence.it/en/node/12061) |

The city record had the station from 1905 to 1860 (an impossible range); the model replaces it, from 1848.

## Review list
