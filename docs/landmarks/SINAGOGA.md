## What the model shows (from photographs)
Photographs (credited on the [library page](/library/sinagoga/)): Txllxt TxllxT 2010 from Via Farini, Yair Haklai 2008.
- The hall as one block in bands of white travertine and pink stone, tall horseshoe windows on the sides, a stone cornice.
- The front between two banded towers with small copper domes: a portico of three horseshoe arches and a round window.
- The drum with sixteen windows and the great dome in green copper, its top ≈46 m.
