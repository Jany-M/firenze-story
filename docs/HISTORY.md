### Before Florentia – what the app shows (v0.3, inferred)
- Villages at Orsanmichele (13 stilt huts, 2 kilns, livestock pen), Badia–Bargello (3 huts, from c. 720 BC) and Costa San Giorgio (5 huts on the hill); hearths and kilns smoke; flocks grazing on the fields.
- Necropoleis: 16 cremation pit graves with biconical urns (via Strozzi – Porta Rossa); 4 horseshoe stelae of the Fiesolan type (Piazza della Libertà, from c. 600 BC).
- River: dugouts carrying jars up and down the Arno; in Live mode people wade across the ford. No ferry and no bridge before the Roman crossing.
- Live mode: villagers at hearths and kilns, a shepherd, paddlers, people at the ford (period clothing is still the generic ancient tunic/cloak).
- Not shown yet: the three branches of the Arno and the marsh, Fiesole on its hill, cypress windbreaks and vineyards as distinct crops.

## D. Daily-life vignettes (procedural, per era)

- Merchant ↔ customer at a stall: Mercato Vecchio (to 1885), Mercato Nuovo (1547+), San Lorenzo (1874+)
- Butchers, then goldsmiths, on Ponte Vecchio (before / after 1593)
- Wool workers and dyers (Oltrarno, S. Croce *tiratoi*), carrying cloth to the Arno
- People entering and leaving churches at the bells (Mass schedule), confraternity processions (Misericordia, with its black hoods, from 1244)
- Notaries and bankers at the *banchi* (Orsanmichele / Mercato Nuovo), guild members at their halls (*Arti*)
- Water carriers, mules and carts at the gates, tolls (*gabella*) at Porta Romana / San Gallo / Prato
- Roman era: forum, baths, theatre audiences, legionary veterans farming the centuriation
- 19th c.: horse trams, carriages, *fiaccherai*; 20th c.: bicycles, trams, cars, Vespa; today: tourists
