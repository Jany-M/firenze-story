# Refinement list

Everything still to refine on the landmark models, gathered from the review lists of each technical page (the pages
stay the reference; this list is regenerated from them by `pipeline/53_refinements.py`). Statues and fountains have their
own list: [STATUES.md](STATUES.md). Landmarks not modelled yet are in the plan (`_dev/notes/PROJECT-DRAFT.md` §12).

## Bargello – landmark model
Technical page: [BARGELLO.md](landmarks/BARGELLO.md)

- [ ] Photo comparison still to do from fixed viewpoints (Via del Proconsolo, Piazza San Firenze, Via Ghibellina, the courtyard); window rhythm, the portal and the Ghibellina bifore are generic.
- [ ] The staircase side and the exact Verone span are read from the LiDAR heights (*inferred*): check on photos.
- [ ] Courtyard details missing: octagonal pillars, the Podestà coats of arms on the walls, the lion on the newel post.
- [ ] Prison years 1574–1857 (walled windows, partitions) and the 1857–65 restoration are not shown.
- [ ] The houses that stood on the eastern part of the block before 1316 are not modelled (the ground is open).

## Campanile and Baptistery – landmark models
Technical page: [CAMPANILE-BAPTISTERY.md](landmarks/CAMPANILE-BAPTISTERY.md)

- [ ] Campanile: relief panels are geometric stand-ins (no figures); the niches hold blocks, not statues; the three storeys of the Talenti part are equal in pattern; the door on the north side is not modelled.
- [ ] Baptistery: the lower order's geometric marble patterns are simplified to framed panels; window aedicules are generic; the bronze statue groups above the doors (Rustici, Danti, Sansovino) and the porphyry columns are missing; the earlier church on the site (before 1059) is not modelled.

## Corridoio Vasariano (the Vasari Corridor)
Technical page: [CORRIDOIO-VASARIANO.md](landmarks/CORRIDOIO-VASARIANO.md)

- [ ] Places along the run are read off the DSM profile and photographs: the two Via de' Bardi arches, the Arco dei Rossi and the portico (s ≈ 124–152 m from the bridge) are *inferred*; the 1565 arch on Via de' Bardi (8 m) is the 1950 width minus 4 m.
- [ ] The Lungarno loggia has 19 equal bays; the real arches and the corner over the bridge head may differ.
- [ ] The corridor inside the Uffizi and its stretch over San Pier Scheraggio are not drawn apart from the Uffizi model.
- [ ] Before 1565 the Lungarno site is left empty (the houses there are not reconstructed).
- [ ] The end at Boboli stops where OSM stops, by the Grotta del Buontalenti; the last stretch into Palazzo Pitti is not drawn.
- [ ] Torre dei Mannelli: size (7 m square) and openings are simplified.
- [ ] The Uffizi's west end on the Lungarno is a plain block (windows on a regular grid, ground-floor arches *inferred*); check against photographs.

## Santa Maria del Fiore (Duomo) – landmark model
Technical page: [DUOMO.md](landmarks/DUOMO.md)

- [ ] Revetment pattern is a regular grid per bay; the real panels vary per bay and period (Arnolfo/Talenti vs 19th c.).
- [ ] Façade statues, mosaics and the many small pinnacles/aedicules are simplified to niches and gables.
- [ ] Windows are drawn on the wall face (no depth); tracery simplified; Porta della Mandorla relief not modelled.
- [ ] Dome tiles follow world-projected texture (courses not aligned to each sail); dome oculi and the marble "occhi" of the sails are missing; drum gallery only on the SE face (correct) but simplified.
- [ ] Campanile and Baptistery are still LiDAR shells (next landmarks).

## Loggia dei Lanzi – landmark model

- [ ] Piers are plain blocks: the real ones are clustered (composite) piers with Gothic capitals; vaults are simplified to ribs under a flat soffit.

## Ponte Vecchio – landmark model
Technical page: [PONTE-VECCHIO.md](landmarks/PONTE-VECCHIO.md)

- [ ] Shop widths, heights and colours are randomised within the measured envelope, not surveyed shop by shop.
- [ ] The three large windows over the central arcade are not modelled. The "1939" given here before follows the story that Mussolini opened them for Hitler's visit (May 1938); it.wikipedia calls that a legend (central opening 1860, side ones only plastered in 1938) – see [CORRIDOIO-VASARIANO.md](landmarks/CORRIDOIO-VASARIANO.md#where-the-sources-disagree).
- [ ] The clock and sundial, the inscriptions of the 1333 flood and the padlock fence around the bust are missing.
- [ ] 1944: the bridge was spared, but its approaches were mined; the destroyed blocks at both ends are handled by the city records, not by this model.
