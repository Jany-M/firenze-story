# The Synagogue

The Tempio Maggiore, built 1874–1882 by Marco Treves, Mariano Falcini and Vincenzo Micheli with the estate left by David Levi: a front in bands of white travertine and pink stone, two towers and the great green copper dome that stands out over the roofs of Santa Croce.

## What you see
- The front under its great round arch: the portico of three horseshoe arches, paired windows, a three-light window and a rose
- The banded towers with their open belfries and small copper domes
- The cross arms ending in the same great arches; the Moorish cresting along the cornices
- The drum ringed with arched windows and the ribbed green dome
- The garden behind its cast-iron railing, with palms
