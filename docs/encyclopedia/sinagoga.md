# The Synagogue

The Tempio Maggiore, built 1874–1882 by Marco Treves, Mariano Falcini and Vincenzo Micheli with the estate left by David Levi: a front in bands of white travertine and pink stone, two towers and the great green copper dome that stands out over the roofs of Santa Croce.
