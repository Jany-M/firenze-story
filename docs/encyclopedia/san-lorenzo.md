# San Lorenzo

The parish church of the Medici and one of the oldest churches in Florence: consecrated in 393, it served as the city's
cathedral before Santa Reparata. Rebuilt from 1421 by Filippo Brunelleschi with Medici money, it gathers around it the
family's burial places – Brunelleschi's Old Sacristy, Michelangelo's New Sacristy and the domed Cappella dei Principi –
and Michelangelo's Laurentian Library. Its façade has stayed bare brick: Michelangelo designed one for Leo X in 1518, and
it was never built.

## What you see
- A long nave with a tall clerestory, lower aisles and a row of side chapels, under a gabled tile roof; at the crossing a
  low ribbed dome.
- On either side of the transept the two sacristies: the Old Sacristy (south) with its umbrella dome, the New Sacristy
  (north) with Michelangelo's lantern.
- Behind the choir the octagon of the Cappella dei Principi, the grand dukes' mausoleum, with the second largest dome in
  the city after the cathedral's.
- To the south the canons' cloister, and on its west range the long reading room of the Laurentian Library.

## Through time
| Year | What happened |
|---|---|
| 393 | The first church is consecrated, traditionally with Saint Ambrose present |
| 1059 | Enlarged and reconsecrated under Gherardo di Borgogna (later Pope Nicholas II) |
| 1418–21 | Houses are cleared for a new transept; works blessed on 10 August 1421 |
| 1421–28 | Brunelleschi's Old Sacristy; Giovanni di Bicci de' Medici is buried there in 1429 |
| 1457–60 | Antonio Manetti builds the cloister and takes over the church works |
| 1461 | High altar consecrated, the church "almost finished" |
| 1518 | Michelangelo's wooden model for the façade – never built |
| c. 1521–34 | Michelangelo's New Sacristy with the Medici tombs |
| 1524–71 | The Laurentian Library, opened in 1571 |
| 1604 | First stone of the Cappella dei Principi (Matteo Nigetti, after Don Giovanni de' Medici) |
| 1640 / 1743 | The chapel finished (1640) – or its dome only in 1743, depending on the source |
| 1740 | Ferdinando Ruggieri's bell tower |

**Where the sources disagree:** whether the Cappella dei Principi was finished in 1640 or its dome only in 1743 (a window
cartouche on the outside is dated 1642; the exterior was designed by the Ruggieris in the 18th century). The model shows the
dome from 1640. The bell tower is 54 m in it.wikipedia and about 50 m on the 2023 LiDAR.
