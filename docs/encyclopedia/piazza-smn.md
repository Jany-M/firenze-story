# Piazza Santa Maria Novella

The square before Santa Maria Novella, opened from 1287. Its two marble obelisks on bronze tortoises, set up in 1608,
marked the turning posts of the Palio dei Cocchi, the chariot race run on the eve of San Giovanni; wooden pyramids had
done it since 1563. The lawns and benches round them are 20th-century.

## What you see
- The two obelisks: pedestal with red panels, four bronze tortoises, the shaft, a bronze lily on top
- Eight lawns in two ovals with clipped hedges, stone benches beside them
