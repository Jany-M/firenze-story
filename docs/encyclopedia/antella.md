# Palazzo dell'Antella

The palace on the south side of Piazza Santa Croce whose whole front is painted: Giulio Parigi made one house of several
in 1619 for Niccolò dell'Antella, and in 1619–20 Giovanni da San Giovanni and about a dozen painters covered the front with
allegories, framed scenes, putti and garlands – famously in a matter of weeks. The upper floors overhang the square on
stone brackets, as the medieval houses here did.

## What you see
- The painted front, from a straightened photograph: allegories in grisaille between the windows, the coloured frieze, the attic panels
- The bust of Cosimo II in the band under the lower windows
- The overhang on its stone brackets over the shops and the portal with the family arms
