# Palazzo della Borsa

The Chamber of Commerce's palace on the Lungarno Diaz, built in 1858–60 for Grand Duke Leopold II where the wool guild's
drying house stood: Michelangelo Maiorfi's design, reworked by Emilio De Fabris with a pronaos of Doric columns under a
pediment. It held the Exchange, the Chamber of Commerce and the Banca Nazionale Toscana.
