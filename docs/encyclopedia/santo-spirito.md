# Santo Spirito

The Augustinian friars came to Oltrarno in the 13th century and built a church on what became Piazza Santo Spirito. In the
1430s Filippo Brunelleschi designed a new one, a forest of grey stone columns around the whole church; building began in
1444, two years before he died, and the old church burnt down in 1471 while the new one was still rising. It was
consecrated in 1481. Its front was never finished: it stayed bare stone until it was plastered in the 18th century, and
it is still the plain, curving front that closes the square today.

## What you see
- A plain plastered front with three doors, a round window and a curving gable, at the end of a square with trees and a fountain.
- Behind it Brunelleschi's dome over the crossing and the slender bell tower by Baccio d'Agnolo.
- Giuliano da Sangallo's octagonal sacristy beside the church, and the convent's two cloisters behind it.
- Inside (not in the model): the nave lined with chapels all round.
- To the left, the Cenacolo, the friars' old refectory with Orcagna's fresco of the Last Supper.

## The cloisters
The Augustinian convent has two cloisters, each with its own card in the model.
- **Chiostro dei Morti**, the smaller one by the church, was rebuilt by Giulio and Alfonso Parigi around 1620: round arches
  on columns, plain windows above and a basin in the middle of the lawn.
- **Chiostro Grande**, Bartolomeo Ammannati's great cloister, was built in 1564–69, with wide arches on Tuscan columns and
  round-headed windows between pilaster strips above.

## Through time
| Year | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1250–52 | Land given to the Augustinians; a first church dedicated |
| 1269 | The Augustinians start a larger church |
| 1444 | Brunelleschi's church is begun |
| 1471 | The old church burns down |
| 1481 | The new church is consecrated, with its dome |
| 1489 | Giuliano da Sangallo's sacristy is begun |
| 1503–70 | The bell tower |
| 18th century | The bare stone front is plastered |
