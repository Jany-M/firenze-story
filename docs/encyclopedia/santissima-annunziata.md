# Piazza della Santissima Annunziata

Florence's most harmonious square grew around one idea. In 1419 Filippo Brunelleschi began a portico for the foundling
hospital, the Innocenti: light round arches on slim grey columns, the first building of the Renaissance. A century later
the Servite friars built the same portico on the opposite side, and in 1601 the church at the end got a third one, its
columns set higher so that all the arches line up. Ferdinando I rides in the middle, between Tacca's sea-monster fountains.

## What you see
- On the south-east side, the Spedale degli Innocenti: nine arches on a long flight of steps, a window over each arch,
  and Andrea della Robbia's blue tondi with babies in swaddling clothes.
- Opposite, the Loggia dei Servi di Maria, a 16th-century copy of the same portico, now a hotel.
- At the end, the portico of the Santissima Annunziata, the Servites' church, with Michelozzo's round domed tribune behind it.
- In the middle, the bronze Grand Duke Ferdinando I on horseback (Giambologna and Tacca, 1608) and two fountains with sea
  monsters (Tacca, set up in 1641).

## The Chiostrino dei Voti
Between the portico and the church there is a small square courtyard, begun in 1447 to a design by Michelozzo. Its name
comes from the wax ex-votos that pilgrims hung there for centuries. In the 1510s Andrea del Sarto, Pontormo and Rosso
Fiorentino painted its walls, which made it a cradle of the new Mannerist style. Under Leopold II it was covered with an
iron and glass roof to keep the weather off the frescoes; in the model it has its own card.

## Through time
| Year | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1250 | The Servites lay the first stone of their church (25 March) |
| 1419 | Brunelleschi's portico for the Innocenti is begun |
| 1444 | Michelozzo starts the church's round tribune |
| 1445 | The Innocenti open their doors (25 January) |
| 1477 | The tribune is inaugurated; the nave is finished in 1481 |
| 1487 | Andrea della Robbia's tondi on the Innocenti |
| 1516–25 | The Loggia dei Servi di Maria |
| 1601 | Caccini's portico in front of the church |
| 1608 | The statue of Ferdinando I |
| 1641 | Tacca's fountains are set up |
| 1720 | The last three arches of the Loggia dei Servi are walled up |
| 1875 | The Innocenti's foundling window is closed |

In the app the Innocenti appear in the 1420s, the church's middle arch in 1460 and the full portico in 1601, the Loggia dei
Servi in 1525. Details and open points: [technical page](/docs/landmarks/ANNUNZIATA/).
