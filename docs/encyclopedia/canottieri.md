# Società Canottieri Firenze

Florence's oldest rowing club, with its rooms in the Uffizi's old vaults and its lawn on the Arno's bank below the gallery,
since 1933, looking up the river to the Ponte Vecchio. A green door on the Lungarno Anna Maria Luisa de' Medici leads down to it.

- The lawn on the bank with green iron chairs and tables, roses along the wall
