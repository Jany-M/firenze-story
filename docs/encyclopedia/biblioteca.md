# Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze

The national library, on Piazza dei Cavalleggeri beside Santa Croce: Cesare Bazzani won the competition in 1906, the
first stone was laid in 1911, and the books came from the Uffizi in 1935. Its front in pietra forte has a portico of three
arches between two small towers holding statues of Dante and Galileo. On 4 November 1966 the flood drowned its basements –
the volunteers who dug out the books were called the *angeli del fango*.

## What you see
- The portico of three arches, the arms of Florence and Savoy, the name panel
- The two towers with their bronze statues
- The wings in pietra forte with three storeys of windows
- The rotunda on Via Magliabechi, the first part finished
