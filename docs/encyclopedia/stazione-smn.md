# Santa Maria Novella station

The station of 1935 by Giovanni Michelucci and the Gruppo Toscano: a long, low building of pietra forte, kept horizontal so as not to clash with Santa Maria Novella, its glass "cascade" between two stone blocks, a flat canopy along its front and its east side, and the Palazzina Reale in white marble for the king's visits. It replaced the Maria Antonia station of 1848, which stood nearer the church.

## What you see
- The clock block, the cascade of frosted glass and the high blind block over the canopy
- The long stone walls in even courses; the taxi-rank canopy along Via Valfonda
- The platforms under flat canopies and the tracks running north-west
- The Palazzina Reale with its portico of honour and Griselli's marble group
- Before 1935: the Maria Antonia station with its clock turret and train shed
