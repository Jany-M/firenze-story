# Santa Maria Novella station

The station of 1935 by Giovanni Michelucci and the Gruppo Toscano: a long, low building of pietra forte, kept horizontal so as not to clash with Santa Maria Novella, with its glazed cascade over the entrance. It replaced the Maria Antonia station of 1848, which stood nearer the church.
