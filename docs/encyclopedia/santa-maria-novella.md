# Santa Maria Novella

The Dominicans' church, west of the old city. It has a Gothic interior with Masaccio's Trinity, Ghirlandaio's
Tornabuoni chapel and Brunelleschi's crucifix, and a façade begun in Gothic marble and finished by Leon Battista Alberti.

## Through time

| Year | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1221 | The Dominicans receive the small church of Santa Maria delle Vigne. |
| 1279 | The new church is begun from the east end. Fra Sisto and Fra Ristoro are named among its builders. |
| 1330s | Fra Jacopo Talenti's bell tower. |
| c. 1357–60 | The nave is finished. The lower façade is built in white and green marble, with the *avelli* (family tombs). |
| 1365–67 | Andrea di Bonaiuto frescoes the Spanish Chapel. |
| c. 1427 | Masaccio's Trinity. |
| 1458–1470 | Alberti completes the façade for Giovanni Rucellai: the central portal, the attic, the temple front and the volutes. The Rucellai sail is carved in the frieze. |
| 1485–90 | Ghirlandaio's frescoes in the Tornabuoni chapel. |
