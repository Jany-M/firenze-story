# Teatro del Maggio Musicale Fiorentino

The opera house of the Maggio Musicale, by Paolo Desideri (ABDR), inaugurated on 21 December 2011: a white block for the great hall and a dark fly tower set on a sloping stone base whose roofs are public space – the square, terraces and an open-air cavea.

## What you see
- The white block of the great hall over the glazed foyer with the red letters of MAGGIO
- The dark fly tower in glazed terracotta
- The stone base sloping down to the square, walked over to the terraces
- The cavea on the roof, facing the fly tower's wall
