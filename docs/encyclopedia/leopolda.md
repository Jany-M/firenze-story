# Stazione Leopolda

Florence's first railway terminus, opened in 1848 for the line to Livorno and named after Grand Duke Leopold II. Passenger trains left it for the central station in 1860; it housed the National Exhibition of 1861, then workshops, and today hosts events.

## What you see
- The rusticated end block with three great arches where the tracks left for Livorno
- The long shed under three gabled roofs, with round windows under the eaves
- The head towards Porta al Prato: the portico of seven arches of the 1861 Exhibition (with its pediment and statues in the first years)
- Today: the forecourt's steel pergola
